## 1. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) Section

### Q1: What is the ITIS Guardian Network?
*Proposal:*  
The ITIS Guardian Network is a sovereign child-safety infrastructure designed specifically for South African schools, parents, and emergency response services. It unites real-time verified school attendance, safe-corridor transit monitoring, and human-in-the-loop emergency response coordination into a single zero-trust platform.

### Q2: How does ITIS protect personal data under South African law (POPIA)?
*Proposal:*  
ITIS strictly adheres to the Protection of Personal Information Act (Act 4 of 2013). All learner and guardian records are stored in sovereign South African data boundaries with role-based access control, cryptographic hashing, and automated PII minimisation. Public-facing surfaces such as the Smart ID QR codes contain zero raw personal or medical information.  
**[DRAFT — NEEDS OWNER CONFIRMATION]**

### Q3: Who operates the First Responder dispatch system?
*Proposal:*  
ITIS operates a Human-in-the-Loop 24/7 Command Centre model. No automated emergency alerts trigger armed dispatch without verified controller confirmation. First Responder units enrolled on the network represent accredited private security partners, community policing forums (CPFs), and municipal emergency services operating in active school safety corridors.  
**[DRAFT — NEEDS OWNER CONFIRMATION]**

### Q4: How does a school join the ITIS Guardian Network?
*Proposal:*  
Schools participate through verified institutional onboarding. The school principal or designated administrative authority registers the institution, validates learner enrolment rolls, and issues tamper-resistant Smart ID cards to registered learners.
